Who can apply?

LMO jobs have no nationality restriction, except that US military members / civilian employees / their families cannot apply (MLC), and US citizens cannot apply to IHA positions.

Non-Japanese applicants need a residence card showing "就労制限なし" (no work restriction). A spouse-of-Japanese-national visa (日本人の配偶者等) has no work restriction.

English certificates are mandatory where a language level is listed — self-declared ability is not accepted. You must attach a copy of a TOEIC / Eiken / TOEFL certificate.

About Japanese

LMO postings do not have a standard "Japanese level" field. This app flags a job only when Japanese is explicitly mentioned in the text. No flag does not guarantee Japanese is unnecessary — the application form itself (履歴書) must be filled in both Japanese and English.

Source

Data is collected weekly from the LMO (駐留軍等労働者労務管理機構) public job site, Yokota branch. Closed postings are kept so we can see how the market looks over time.
